Frequently asked questions

Do I pay Le Groenland a fee?

No. We are direct agent partners of our network hospitals and charge patients no consultancy or advisory fee.

How fast will I get a treatment plan?

Expect a plan within 48 hours of submitting your medical reports.

What should I send first?

Your latest medical reports, scans and test results, plus a short note on your condition. Your home doctor can help.

Can I be treated in my own country?

Yes. We offer options locally or internationally, whichever suits your case and budget.

Do you arrange travel?

Yes. We handle visa processing, flights, accommodation and airport pick-up and drop-off.

What happens after treatment?

We provide post-procedure summaries for you and your home doctor and guide you through follow-up.
