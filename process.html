Step-by-Step Care

From first message to follow-up

Our dedicated patient coordinators manage the administrative and logistical burden so you can concentrate entirely on your health.

  1. 1

    Send reports

    Upload your recent medical history, doctor notes, laboratory tests, and MRI or CT scans via our secure portal or WhatsApp.

  2. 2

    Get a plan in 48h

    Specialists at our partner hospitals evaluate your case. You receive customized treatment options and itemized hospital estimates within 48 hours.

  3. 3

    Choose a hospital

    Consult with our coordinator and your hometown doctor. Decide on the clinical team, location, and budget that best meet your expectations.

  4. 4

    Travel coordinated

    We process your medical visa, book flexible flights, arrange companion accommodation near the hospital, and schedule airport transfers.

  5. 5

    Recover with support

    Undergo care with bedside coordinator assistance. Receive a comprehensive discharge summary and medication plan for your local doctor.

Full Logistics Handled

Travel support included

International travel for medical reasons requires specialized attention. We coordinate all logistics so patients and family companions travel comfortably and safely.

  • Official medical visa invitation letters and embassy support
  • Patient-friendly accommodation selected for accessibility and comfort
  • Private airport pick-up, hospital check-in, and transfer shuttles
  • Language interpretation services (English, French, Arabic, and local dialects)
