Our Story & Mission

About Le Groenland

Over a decade of dedicated service connecting Sub-Saharan African patients and families to the world's finest medical centers.

Building Healthcare Bridges

Medical travel, made manageable

Our mission is to transform the medical travel experience for Sub-Saharan African patients seeking treatment options. With over a decade of dedicated service, we began by helping patients find quality care in the UAE and guiding them through what can be a complex international healthcare system.

Today we partner with 100+ internationally accredited hospitals across 20+ countries so patients can access world-class diagnostics, oncology, cardiology, orthopaedics, and specialist surgeries. We are more than a company—we are a trusted bridge to health, comfort, and peace of mind.
